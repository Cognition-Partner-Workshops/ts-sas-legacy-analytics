## Blocking gaps for cutover, in the auditor's words

1. The pack proves that the Databricks pipeline reproduces *a Python reading* of the SAS programs on one frozen day of seed data. It proves nothing about what the SAS estate actually emits on production volumes. Until the customer runs the same day through SAS and hands over the CSVs (REQ-05), "GREEN" means "self-consistent", not "correct". That is the DEGRADED caveat, and approving through it must be an explicit written choice.
2. Nobody can read the result. The target catalog has no grants and no principal other than the migrating admin identity; no downstream consumer, report recipient or e-mail destination has been named. There is no flip to rehearse.
3. The regulatory numbers rest on constants the source itself labels placeholders. A wrong `capital_adequacy` that is reference-equal is still wrong.
4. Failure is silent: no destination, no `on_failure`, and the scheduled trigger has never fired for either job.
5. Two requester decisions are outstanding and cannot be made by the migration: DEC-016 (exception-history semantics) and acceptance or closure of the seed-unexercised divergences (T-9 x2, AMB-06/07/08, `ABORT_ON_ERR=N`, repair-run).

None of these is an engineering defect in the converted code found by this audit; all are inputs only the requester or the customer can supply.

## Assessment of the STOP E brief

The brief's recommendation (DEFER; approve only by explicitly accepting the DEGRADED caveat) follows from the evidence. Its seven blocking gaps are each traceable to primary evidence verified above. It understates two things: (a) the unexercised list (F-5) and (b) the run-history completeness (F-4). Neither changes the recommendation. The decision sentences are unambiguous and the approve sentence correctly binds acceptance of the caveat and of gaps 2-7 as post-cutover items.

No job was triggered, no bundle deployed, no table written, no grant issued, no legacy file read for modification. `SHOW GRANTS` was not re-executed (the pack's same-day listing was accepted). Converted code under `databricks/src` was not executed beyond the unit-test suite; the AMB-06 reading was confirmed by inspection only (F-5).

DEGRADED mode: reference-derived, not SAS-produced.
